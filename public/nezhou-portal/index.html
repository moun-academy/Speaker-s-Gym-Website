YOUR PROFESSIONAL VOCABULARY TOOLKIT

Words you can put to work.

Useful language for meetings and interviews. Pick one or two words, understand the meaning and try them in your own sentences. Clear language matters more than impressive jargon.

Make your point 4 useful words +
Clarify
Make something easier to understand.Could you clarify the main priority?
Prioritize
Decide what needs attention first.I would prioritize the most urgent issue.
Align
Make sure people or plans work toward the same goal.Let’s align on the next steps.
Recommend
Suggest a course of action with a reason.My recommendation is [action], because [reason].
Discuss decisions 4 useful words +
Trade-off
A benefit you accept alongside a cost or disadvantage.The trade-off is [benefit] versus [cost].
Feasibility
Whether a plan is practical and achievable.We need to assess the feasibility of this timeline.
Mitigate
Reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.We could mitigate that risk by [action].
Evidence-based
Supported by reliable information.I would make an evidence-based recommendation.
Work with people 4 useful words +
Stakeholder
Someone affected by or involved in a decision.Which stakeholders should we involve?
Cross-functional
Involving people from different departments.This would need a cross-functional discussion.
Consensus
A shared agreement within a group.Let’s check whether we have consensus on the next step.
Address
Deal with an issue directly.I would first address the main concern.
Explain your contribution 4 useful words +
Ownership
Taking responsibility for a task and its follow-through.I took ownership of [specific task].
Initiative
Acting on a useful idea without waiting to be asked.I took the initiative to [real action].
Streamline
Make a process simpler or more efficient.One opportunity would be to streamline [process].
Outcome
The result of an action or decision.The outcome was [specific result I can support].

Examples are practice templates. Replace bracketed text with your own details and describe only experience and results that are true for you.
